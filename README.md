# RIPPLE — Life & Markets

**A life simulation game where everyday decisions and financial choices shape your future.**

RIPPLE is a browser-based life simulation game that combines personal life management with stock market investing. Experience the passage of time, manage your resources, make decisions, and explore how your choices influence your virtual life.

## Features

- **Life Simulation:** Manage your character's health, energy, happiness, and finances.
- **Historical Market Simulation:** Explore real historical stock price data across randomly selected time periods, making every new game session a different experience.
- **Year-Based Market Context:** Stock charts display the corresponding years, allowing players to consider real-world news and major events that influenced market movements as they progress through the game and gain realistic experience.
- **No Future Spoilers:** Technically, you *could* look up what happened to a stock next online... but where's the fun in that? 👀 Play fair, embrace the uncertainty, and let your financial instincts do the work!
-**Portfolio Management:** Track your investments, selling stocks and financial progress in market.
- **Career & Income:** Work to earn money, invest and manage your available cash by facing real time incidents like damage to laptop and having to pay amount. user also faces real life decisions like marriage and buying a car.
- **Time Progression:** Advance through the game month by month and observe changes over time.
- **Life Activities:** Use activities such as resting and travelling to navigate your virtual life.
- **Interactive Interface:** Explore the game through a dedicated dashboard, navigation panels, and a 3D environment.

## Built With

- **HTML5** — Structure of the application
- **CSS3** — Styling and responsive interface
- **JavaScript** — Game logic and interactions
- **Three.js** — 3D environment and visual rendering
- **JSON** — Structured data for market and life-related information

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

You need a modern web browser and an internet connection to load external libraries.

### Run Locally

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/neelsawarn/Syntax-Error.git
   ```

2. Open the project folder:

   ```bash
   cd Syntax-Error
   ```

3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

   Alternatively, open the folder in Visual Studio Code and use the **Live Server** extension to run the project locally.

No Node.js installation is required for this basic browser-based setup.

## How to Play

1. Launch the game in your browser.
2. Explore the interface and review your character's financial and personal status.
3. Use the available actions to manage your time and resources.
4. Visit the stock market and portfolio sections to explore investments.
5. Advance the simulation and observe how your decisions affect your progress.

## Project Goal

RIPPLE explores the relationship between financial decisions, personal well-being, and long-term life outcomes through an interactive simulation.

The project aims to make financial concepts and decision-making more engaging by placing them in the context of a virtual life.
